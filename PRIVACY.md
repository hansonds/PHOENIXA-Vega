# PHOENIXA Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

PHOENIXA ("the app") is a client for Jellyfin media servers on Fire TV devices with Vega OS. It is developed by Hanson ([phoenicx.org](https://phoenicx.org)) as an independent project. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

**PHOENIXA does not collect, sell or share any personal data with the developer or with any third party.** It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no developer-run servers.

## What the app stores on your device

To keep you signed in and remember your settings, the app saves the following **only on your TV device**:

- The address of the Jellyfin server you enter
- Your Jellyfin username and the sign-in token your server issues (your password is **not** stored)
- Your app settings (for example theme, card size, playback options, and your preferred audio and subtitle languages)
- Pictures (posters and artwork) that the system may keep for a short time so pages load faster
- If the app ever crashes, a short technical description of the error, shown only to you in Settings

This data never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects **only to the Jellyfin server you choose**, and to the places that server sends videos from (for example TorBox, when your server uses the Torvia plugin). It sends your sign-in details to your server, along with requests to browse and play your media, and your watch progress (so you can continue on other devices). That server is run by you or by whoever invited you, and its own privacy practices apply. The developer of PHOENIXA has no access to it.

The optional "Test the player" button in Settings plays a short public sample video from Amazon's sample content server. No personal information is sent with it.

## Data deletion

All data is held on your device and on the Jellyfin server you use. To delete it, sign out or uninstall the app. Data held on your Jellyfin server can be removed by that server's administrator.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
